Core Features

Everything your community needs

From real-time monitoring to AI-powered insights — VitalNet covers the full spectrum of community healthcare.

Real-Time Vitals

Continuous monitoring of heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, temperature and respiratory rate with instant anomaly detection.

AI Health Assistant

Conversational AI that interprets symptoms, answers health queries, and provides actionable guidance 24/7.

Disease Risk Prediction

Machine learning models assess diabetes, hypertension, and cardiac risk with explainable probability scores.

Emergency Alerts

Automated critical alerts with GPS dispatch, SMS/call notifications to family and nearest healthcare facility.

Population Analytics

Community-wide health trends, disease prevalence maps, outbreak early warning, and resource planning dashboards.

Secure & Private

HIPAA-aligned data handling, end-to-end encryption, role-based access control, and full audit trails.

Workflow

How VitalNet Works

01

IoT Sensor Collection

Wearable and bedside sensors capture vitals continuously and transmit via Wi-Fi / LoRa.

02

Secure Data Ingestion

Encrypted payloads land in the cloud pipeline; data is validated, timestamped, and stored.

03

AI Analysis & Risk Scoring

ML models analyse trends, detect anomalies, and score disease risk in milliseconds.

04

Alert & Response

Critical findings trigger multi-channel alerts to doctors, family, and emergency services instantly.
