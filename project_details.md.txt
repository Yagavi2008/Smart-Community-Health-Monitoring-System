Smart Community Health Monitoring System

Project Overview

The Smart Community Health Monitoring System is a web-based application developed to monitor, analyze, and manage the health status of individuals within a community. The system enables healthcare providers and administrators to track health records, monitor health trends, and identify potential health risks in real time.

This project improves healthcare accessibility, enhances disease prevention, and provides a centralized platform for community health management.

---

Problem Statement

Traditional community health monitoring often relies on manual record-keeping and fragmented healthcare systems, which can lead to delayed responses, inaccurate data, and difficulties in tracking health trends. Communities require an efficient and reliable system to monitor health conditions and support preventive healthcare measures.

The Smart Community Health Monitoring System addresses these challenges by providing an intelligent and user-friendly health management solution.

---

Objectives

- Monitor community health records efficiently.
- Track health conditions in real time.
- Improve healthcare accessibility.
- Identify potential health risks early.
- Support data-driven healthcare decisions.
- Enhance preventive healthcare services.

System Modules

1. User Management

Handles registration, authentication, and profile management for citizens, healthcare providers, and administrators.

2. Health Monitoring

Tracks health information and monitors health conditions in real time.

3. Medical Record Management

Maintains and updates patient health records securely.

4. Health Analytics

Analyzes health trends and generates reports for better decision-making.

5. Database Management

Stores health records, user information, and healthcare data securely.
