Our mission

Healthcare that reaches the whole community, not just the clinic.

VitalNet was built as an SIH 2025 prototype to close the gap between individual health data and community-level care — giving members, doctors and admins a shared, real-time picture of wellbeing.

Why we built this

Rural and semi-urban communities often catch chronic conditions late. Continuous, simple monitoring plus AI-guided nudges can flag risk long before a hospital visit is needed.

Privacy first

Health data is sensitive by nature. Access is role-based — members control their own records; doctors and admins only see what their role requires.

Built for teams

Community members, doctors, and health administrators each get a workspace tailored to their day-to-day decisions.

How it works

From a single vital reading to a community insight

1. Record

Members log vitals or connect a device — BP, sugar, SpO₂, temperature, BMI.

2. Analyse

The AI engine screens for abnormal readings and disease risk patterns.

3. Alert

Doctors and emergency contacts are notified the moment something looks off.

4. Improve

Admins track community trends to plan camps, vaccination drives, and outreach.
